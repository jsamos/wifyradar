## 3. Description (4000 characters max)

```
wifyradar keeps shared lists in sync for a small group — groceries, todos, errands, or anything else you track together.

Create a list, add items, and check them off. Invite someone with their Apple ID email; when they accept, both of you see the same list update over iCloud.

Sharing stays private to the people you invite. Contacts access is optional and only helps pick an email on your device; your address book is not uploaded.

Requires an iPhone signed into iCloud. Free to use; list content lives in your iCloud account via CloudKit.
```

**Done when:** Description is filled.
