Support

wifyradar is a shared lists app for iPhone. Lists and items stay in sync through your iCloud account.

Help with sharing

  • Both people need to be signed into iCloud on their iPhones.
  • Invite with the other person’s Apple ID email, then accept the share on their device.
  • Contacts access is optional and only helps pick that email on your device.

Contact

Email admin@vesenterprises.com with the device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue.
