Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the wifyradar iPhone app (“the App”) handles information. The App is provided by the independent developer of wifyradar (“we,” “us”).

What the App stores

List names, items, and related list content you create in the App are stored in your iCloud account using Apple CloudKit. We do not operate a separate server that holds that content. Apple provides iCloud storage and sync under Apple’s terms and privacy policy.

Sharing and Apple ID email

When you invite someone to a shared list, the App uses their Apple ID email (and related CloudKit participant identity) so Apple can deliver the share invitation and keep participants in sync. People you share with can see content on lists they can access.

Contacts

Contacts access is optional. If you allow it, the App uses Contacts only on this device to help you pick an Apple ID email when inviting someone. Your address book is not uploaded to our servers. Only the email you select is used for the CloudKit invite.

Account and identifiers

The App relies on your Apple ID / iCloud account for sync and sharing. We do not create a separate wifyradar login. Push notifications used for sync are delivered through Apple’s systems tied to your device and iCloud setup.

What we do not collect

The App does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs that track you across other companies’ apps. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

  • Stop sharing a list or remove people in the App’s sharing controls.
  • Leave a shared list you do not own (participant delete / leave).
  • Delete lists you own in the App; after sync, that removes them for participants.
  • Manage or delete iCloud data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud, including options that affect apps using iCloud.
  • Revoke Contacts permission in iOS Settings → wifyradar.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the App to store information from children in a way that violates applicable law.

Changes

We may update this policy when the App’s data practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
