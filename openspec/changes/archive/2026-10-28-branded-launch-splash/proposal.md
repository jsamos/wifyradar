# Proposal: Branded launch splash

## Intent

On cold start / boot, show a branded splash (dark background, gradient **W**, wordmark **wifyradar**, tagline **Stay synced. Stay happy**) before Lists home becomes interactive — matching approved mock option C in [`docs/mocks/launch-splash.html`](../../../docs/mocks/launch-splash.html).

## Scope

Amends **branded-scaffold**. **lists-home** stays the first interactive root after the splash.

- Black system launch screen background (no white flash)
- In-app splash matching mock C, then transition to Lists home
- Tagline copy frozen as approved

## Out of scope

- Making splash the only screen again
- Options A/B from the mock
- Animated marketing video / Lottie
